Learn MCP with Phoebe

Two tracks into the protocol wiring AI to everything

The Model Context Protocol is how AI apps reach your tools and data - one open standard, Linux Foundation governed, adopted by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Microsoft. A leader track teaches you to judge, buy, and govern MCP without touching code. A builder track takes you from reading raw protocol traffic to building, connecting, securing, and publishing a real Python MCP server for Daybreak, a coffee chain. Every builder page has a live wire inspector - step through the actual JSON-RPC messages in your browser.
